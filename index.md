I am Shazny, but you can call me Shaz. 

I am an adjunct lecturer at [Temasek Polytechnic](https://www.tp.edu.sg/), I teach the core *Sustainability and Climate Action* module to first-year diploma students.

I am also a researcher. My research studies how religious and corporate actors rationalise and implement sustainability; and the legal, political and sociological challenges therefrom. I previously served research stints at the [NUS Faculty of Law](https://law.nus.edu.sg/).

Beyond academia, I serve on the panel of volunteer mediators of the [Syariah Court of Singapore](https://cm.syariahcourt.gov.sg/Home) and is an active member with [Nature Society Singapore](https://nss.org.sg/), [Singapore Heritage Society](https://www.instagram.com/sgheritagesociety/?hl=en) and [Al-Mizan Singapore](https://www.almizan.sg/).

I received my [LL.B.](https://law1a.nus.edu.sg/admissions/4_yrs_llb_prog.html) (2018) and [M.Sc. (Environmental Management)](https://scale.nus.edu.sg/programmes/graduate/msc-(environmental-management)) (2024) at the National University of Singapore.
